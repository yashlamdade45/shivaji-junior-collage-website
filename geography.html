Practice Question Bank

Study practice: These original revision questions are not official board questions. Open each answer to self-check and use a labelled map or local example where appropriate.

  1. Name physical and human factors that influence population distribution in India.
    Check answer

    Physical factors include relief, climate, water availability and soils. Human factors include jobs, transport, services, industry and historical settlement. Densities are generally higher where land and services support settlement.

  2. How do monsoon winds affect Indian agriculture?
    Check answer

    Seasonal monsoon rainfall supplies water for rain-fed crops and influences sowing calendars and yields. Uneven or delayed rainfall can cause water stress; very heavy rainfall may cause flooding and crop damage.

  3. Distinguish intensive and extensive farming.
    Check answer

    Intensive farming uses relatively high labour or inputs on a limited area to obtain high output per unit area. Extensive farming uses larger areas with comparatively lower inputs per unit area. Describe the context when choosing examples.

  4. Give two ways transport networks support regional development.
    Check answer

    They connect producers to markets and connect people to jobs and services. Better links can attract investment and reduce travel time, though the benefits depend on fair access and environmental planning.

  5. State one cause and two effects of urbanisation in Maharashtra.
    Check answer

    A cause is migration toward employment, education or services. Effects can include expanded access to jobs and services as well as pressure on housing, transport, water and waste systems. Support claims with a relevant example.

  6. What does a contour line on a topographic map represent?
    Check answer

    It joins points of equal elevation. Close spacing indicates a steep slope; wide spacing indicates a gentler slope, subject to the map's contour interval.

  7. On a map with a scale of 1:50,000, what ground distance does 2 cm represent?
    Check answer

    1 cm represents 50,000 cm (500 m). Therefore 2 cm represents 100,000 cm, or 1 km.

  8. Why is a watershed useful for planning water conservation?
    Check answer

    A watershed is the area draining to a common outlet. Planning at this scale helps identify runoff pathways and coordinate measures such as soil conservation, recharge and storage across the catchment.
