Practice Question Bank

Study practice: These original revision questions are not official board questions. Open each answer to self-check; for longer responses, support points with dates, evidence and context from your text.

  1. How did the Industrial Revolution change urban society and working conditions?
    Check answer

    Mechanised production and factory work encouraged rapid urban growth and wage labour. Cities expanded, while many workers faced long hours, unsafe workplaces and overcrowded housing. Industrialisation also increased production and transport.

  2. How did nineteenth-century social reform movements contribute to education in India?
    Check answer

    Reformers and organisations campaigned against social barriers and promoted schools, including education for girls and marginalised groups. Name relevant people or organisations from your prescribed text and explain their specific contribution.

  3. Give one major cause and one consequence of each World War.
    Check answer

    World War I followed multiple tensions, including militarism, alliances, imperial rivalry and the 1914 assassination at Sarajevo; it ended with major political changes and the Treaty of Versailles. World War II grew from factors including aggressive expansion and unresolved postwar tensions; it ended with enormous loss, the UN's founding and a changed global balance. Expand using your textbook's detail.

  4. How did nationalism affect political change in Asia and Africa?
    Check answer

    Anti-colonial movements mobilised people around self-determination and helped bring independence to many territories. The timing and paths differed by place; use specific movements and dates from your text rather than treating the regions as one case.

  5. Why do historians use both primary and secondary sources?
    Check answer

    Primary sources come from the period being studied, while secondary sources analyse or interpret evidence later. Comparing different sources, checking their origin and context, and considering bias helps build a more reliable account.

  6. State two social effects of industrialisation.
    Check answer

    Examples include migration from rural areas to towns, growth of a wage-earning working class, changes in family and work patterns, and the development of labour movements. Explain the effect rather than listing it alone.

  7. What is meant by a constitutional reform movement?
    Check answer

    It is organised activity seeking changes to political rules, institutions or rights through constitutional means. A strong answer identifies the movement, its demands and the outcome using a studied example.

  8. Why should a historical claim be supported with evidence?
    Check answer

    Evidence allows readers to evaluate how a claim was reached. Cite relevant records or sources, explain how they support the claim, and note limitations or differing interpretations where appropriate.
