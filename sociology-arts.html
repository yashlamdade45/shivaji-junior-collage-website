Practice Question Bank

Study practice: These original revision questions are not official board questions. Open each answer to self-check and use examples carefully, recognising variation between communities.

  1. Define socialisation and name its main agencies.
    Check answer

    Socialisation is the lifelong process through which people learn norms, values, roles and ways of interacting. Common agencies include family, school, peer groups, media and community.

  2. How can urbanisation affect family structure?
    Check answer

    Migration, housing, employment and changing lifestyles may influence household size, residence patterns and care roles. These effects vary; urbanisation does not cause one uniform family form.

  3. Explain one way culture can shape identity.
    Check answer

    Shared language, practices, symbols and values can provide belonging and shape how people understand themselves. Individuals may also hold multiple identities and interpret cultural practices differently.

  4. What is social stratification? Name two bases on which it may occur.
    Check answer

    Social stratification is the structured ranking of people or groups that creates unequal access to resources and opportunities. Class, caste, gender and ethnicity are examples of bases examined in sociology.

  5. Give two ways young people can support social inclusion.
    Check answer

    Challenge discriminatory behaviour respectfully and include people in learning or community activities. Listening to affected groups and supporting equal access also helps.

  6. Distinguish a social norm from a law.
    Check answer

    A norm is a socially shared expectation of behaviour; a law is a formal rule recognised and enforced by the state. Some behaviour can be shaped by both.

  7. What is the difference between a primary and a secondary group?
    Check answer

    A primary group usually involves close, enduring personal relationships, such as a family. A secondary group is generally larger or more task-focused, such as a class or workplace team.

  8. How can social change affect institutions?
    Check answer

    Changes in technology, laws, economy or values can alter institutions' roles and relationships. A strong answer identifies the change, the institution and a supported example.
