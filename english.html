Original English Practice

Study practice: These questions are original practice prompts, not official board questions. Open each answer to self-check.

  1. Choose the correct verb: “Each of the students ___ ready.” (is / are)
    Check answer

    is. “Each” is singular, so it takes a singular verb.

  2. Change to passive voice: “The students completed the project.”
    Check answer

    “The project was completed by the students.” The object becomes the subject; keep the past tense.

  3. Change to reported speech: Riya said, “I am preparing for the test.”
    Check answer

    Riya said that she was preparing for the test. Pronouns and tense change to fit the reporting context.

  4. Correct the sentence: “She do not understand the instruction.”
    Check answer

    “She does not understand the instruction.” Use “does” with the third-person singular subject.

  5. Add punctuation: “when the bell rang the students left the classroom”
    Check answer

    “When the bell rang, the students left the classroom.” Capitalise the opening word and place a comma after the introductory clause.

  6. Identify the figure of speech: “The moon was like a silver coin.”
    Check answer

    Simile: it compares the moon with a silver coin using “like.”

  7. Write a short paragraph on one benefit of regular reading.
    Model answer

    Regular reading helps learners meet new ideas and vocabulary. It can improve comprehension when they read carefully and reflect on a text. Choosing material of interest also makes reading easier to sustain.

  8. What are the essential parts of a formal letter to a school principal?
    Check answer

    Include the sender's address and date, recipient's designation/address, a clear subject, respectful salutation, concise purpose and relevant details, polite closing, and the sender's name/signature. Match the exact format taught in class.
