Original Physics Practice

Study practice: These questions are original practice prompts, not official board questions. Use SI units and show working for numerical problems.

  1. A 4 kg object accelerates at 3 m s⁻². Find the net force.
    Check answer

    By Newton's second law, F = ma = 4 × 3 = 12 N.

  2. A 12 V potential difference is across a 4 Ω resistor. Find the current.
    Check answer

    Ohm's law gives I = V/R = 12/4 = 3 A.

  3. Two 2 Ω resistors are connected in series. Find their equivalent resistance.
    Check answer

    For series resistors, R = R₁ + R₂ = 2 + 2 = 4 Ω.

  4. Find the kinetic energy of a 2 kg body moving at 3 m s⁻¹.
    Check answer

    KE = ½mv² = ½ × 2 × 3² = 9 J.

  5. A wave has frequency 50 Hz and wavelength 2 m. Find its speed.
    Check answer

    v = fλ = 50 × 2 = 100 m s⁻¹.

  6. State Faraday's law of electromagnetic induction and Lenz's law.
    Check answer

    The induced emf is proportional to the rate of change of magnetic flux linkage. Its direction is such that the induced effect opposes the change producing it.

  7. A device operates at 12 V and draws 2 A. Find its electrical power.
    Check answer

    P = VI = 12 × 2 = 24 W.

  8. What does the photoelectric threshold frequency represent?
    Check answer

    It is the minimum frequency of incident radiation needed to eject electrons from a material. Below it, increasing intensity alone does not produce photoelectrons in the standard photoelectric-effect model.
